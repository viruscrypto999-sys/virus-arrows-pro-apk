What is Virus Arrows?

What is Virus Arrows Game?

Virus Arrows Game is an upgraded, high-intensity logic puzzle created by iTzVirus. Your goal is to find unobstructed exit paths, tap snaking arrows in the right topological sequence, and clear the entire grid.

🔥 Virus Pro Mechanics:
• 🔒 Locked Arrows: Clear other arrows first to unlock the chain counter.
• 💣 Virus Bomb Arrows: Clear before the move countdown hits 0!
• ❄️ Ice Frozen Arrows: Slide an adjacent arrow past them to shatter the frost.
• 💣 EMP Blast Booster: Vaporizes any blocking arrow instantly!
